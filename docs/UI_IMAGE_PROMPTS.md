## editorial-ledger

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE beautiful high-fidelity desktop web APPLICATION dashboard screenshot, landscape 1536x1024 composition, flat front view edge-to-edge, no laptop frame or staged scene, no collage. Product: Student Finance Assistant, a real local Python/Streamlit student personal finance application for Indian students. This is an aspirational design concept, not actual app output. Make it achievable with standard web components and excellent graphic design. Sharp legible typography, deliberate spacing, strong hierarchy, professional editorial art direction. Every region must fit inside the image. No stock photos, chatbots, robotic avatars, AI sparkles, crypto, marketing hero, purple gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, huge pill buttons or excessive rounded dashboard cards. Use substantial whitespace but still a functional data-rich product.
Accurate October 2026 data: recorded income ₹15,000; expenses ₹9,900; recorded surplus ₹5,100. Transfers ₹2,000 excluded. Expense breakdown: Bills ₹2,900; Shopping ₹2,000; Education ₹2,000; Food ₹1,400; Entertainment ₹1,000; Transport ₹600. If showing a budget example, Food is ₹1,400 of ₹1,200, ₹200 over budget; exact suggestion: 'Reduce food spending by ₹200 to meet your budget.' Clearly label 'Fictional demo data'. Show navigation Overview, Transactions, Budgets, Categories. Show October 2026 selector and useful 'Add transaction' and 'Import CSV' controls. Realistic INR amounts, good alignment, small elegant monochrome icons, no excessive text. Title 'Your money, clearly.' with short supportive context. Recent transactions can be Hostel rent ₹2,500 Bills; Python course ₹1,500 Education; New shoes ₹1,200 Shopping; Weekly groceries ₹500 Food. Show category review with 'Needs review' and 'Review category' action; model suggestions editable. Never claim bank balance or accuracy. Keep chart and small text clean and plausible.
VISUAL DIRECTION 1: EDITORIAL LEDGER. Warm parchment ivory #F5F2E9 canvas, deep forest ink #173E32, dark near-black text #222B25, muted brass #B49A54 accents. Sophisticated newspaper meets Swiss financial annual report, contemporary and quiet. Use a gorgeous high-contrast serif for the main title and big numerical typography, compact modern sans-serif for labels. Fine hairline separators, small uppercase section labels, minimally rounded panels, elegant vertical forest-green narrow sidebar with cream lettering. Content is light and spacious. Main large expense composition with horizontal bars and elegant ordered category list, right slim column budget status, recent transactions bottom spanning wide. Top summary as three large typography blocks divided by vertical rules rather than floating cards. Tiny project wordmark 'Ledger / student finance'. Add concept label '01 · EDITORIAL LEDGER' subtly in footer. Premium, restrained, exceptionally usable.
